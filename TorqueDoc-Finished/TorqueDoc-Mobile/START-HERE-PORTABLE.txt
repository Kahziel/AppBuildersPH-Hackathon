TORQUEDOC - PORTABLE APP

1. Unzip TorqueDoc-portable.zip to a normal folder, for example Documents.
   Do not run it from inside the zip.
2. Double-click TorqueDoc.exe in the TorqueDoc folder.
   Nothing else needs to be installed. It works offline.
   Tip: right-click TorqueDoc.exe > Show more options > Send to > Desktop
   (create shortcut) to get an icon on your desktop.

The first time, Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the
app is not signed by a company. Click "More info", then "Run anyway".

Closing the TorqueDoc window stops it.

MANUALS
Built-in manuals and saved web pages are in the "library" folder next to
TorqueDoc.exe. See library\README.txt to add more; restart TorqueDoc
afterwards. Newer revisions can also be added inside the app with
"Add an update". Your notes and updates are kept in the "data" folder.
To move to a new version, copy your "data" folder into the new TorqueDoc
folder.

LOCAL AI ANSWERS (optional)
Keyword search works right away. For AI-selected answers, install Ollama
from ollama.com and run once, while online:  ollama pull qwen3:4b

SAFETY
Always open the cited page and check that it applies to your exact machine.
Web pages are labeled "Web" and are not manufacturer documentation. The
Training rig manual is fictional and must never be used for real work.
